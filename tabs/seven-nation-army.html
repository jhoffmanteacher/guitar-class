Hammer-ons and pull-offs
Same box, two new moves. A hammer-on (h): pick the first note, then tap the higher fret hard with your ring finger — two notes, one pick. A pull-off (p): pick the fretted note, then flick that finger off the string so the open string sounds — two notes, one pick. Practice each move slowly until the second note sounds clearly, then add them to your three-note phrases.
