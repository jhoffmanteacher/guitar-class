Mic check

For the teacher, on one class Chromebook. It asks for the mic exactly as the site's games do, shows what the browser really gave, and tests whether automatic volume control (AGC) is acting on the mic.

Nothing is saved or sent anywhere. The numbers stay on this page until you press "Copy results", which puts them on this computer's clipboard only.

2. Ring one note and let it die

Quiet room. Press the button, then pluck ONE open string once, firmly, and let it ring. Do not touch the strings again until the 10 seconds are over.

A guitar note should fall smoothly. If the level climbs back up while the note dies, or jumps in steps, automatic gain is acting. The thresholds below are provisional (first guesses, 2026-11-13): a rise of more than 6 dB inside 200 ms after the pluck, or a climb of more than 10 dB back up from the quietest point, reads as automatic gain; 3 to 6 dB is unclear (run it again).
