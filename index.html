Welcome to Guitar Class! This is your main page for practicing on your own. Here's how it works:

🔑
Signing in
You're in with your Google account. Your progress saves automatically and follows you to any device — laptop, phone, or home computer. Just sign in again and pick up where you left off.
🎯
Finding your work
Pick your Module from the dropdown, then tap a Set. Each set has stations to work through, a few songs to play along with, and a skills checklist at the bottom.
✅
Tracking your progress
As you practice, update each skill: Still working on it while you're learning, then I've got it! once you can do it consistently. No grade, nothing counts against you — it just helps you see how you're growing.
🎧
Practice tools
The Tuner, Timer, and Metronome are the floating buttons in the corner — you'll use them every session. Practicing around other people? Headphones keep the videos clear for you and quiet for everyone else.
📖
Words you'll see everywhere
Level up = an optional harder version of the step. "You've got it when" = the signs you're ready to move on. A lap = one full time through the exercise or loop. BPM = beats per minute — the speed of the metronome.

🌐 Need Spanish? Tap Español at the top anytime.

🔓 Preview mode — set locks are off for you. Students still see them.
